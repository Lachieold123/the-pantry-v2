# The Pantry: Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date of publishing]
**Applies to:** The Pantry app for iPhone, version [2.0.0] and later

## The short version

- The Pantry has **no accounts and no server**. We don't collect your personal information.
- Everything you put into the app (your cupboard, your plan, your shopping list, your recipes and your dish photos) is stored **only on your iPhone**.
- There are **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.
- To delete everything, delete the app.

The rest of this policy explains this in more detail, as the Australian Privacy Principles and Apple ask us to.

We handle personal information in line with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

## 2. What information the app uses, and where it lives

The app keeps the following on your phone, in the app's own private storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

| What | Why the app keeps it |
| --- | --- |
| Your cupboard: the ingredients you've added, and which basics you always have | To show what you can cook with what you have, and to leave those items off your shopping list |
| Your week plan, and your edits to each week's shopping list (ticks, removals, extras) | To build your shopping list and show what's on tonight |
| Your cooking preferences: diet, ingredients to avoid, favourite cuisines, weeknight time | To suggest recipes that suit you |
| Saved recipes, collections, dishes you've marked "Not for us", recently viewed recipes | So you can find them again |
| Your cooked log (what you cooked and when) | To show your kitchen stats and avoid suggesting the same dish too often |
| Recipes you write or import from a web link | So you can plan, shop for and cook them like any other recipe |
| Your plates: photos of dishes you cooked, with their name, notes and details | So you can keep a record of what you've made |
| App settings: theme, units, the Sunday reminder, whether you've seen the tour | So the app works the way you set it |

**About your preferences.** The "Ingredients to avoid" list may show things about your health or beliefs, such as an allergy or a diet. It stays on your phone and we never see it. It is also a convenience for steering suggestions, **not an allergy filter**. Always check the ingredients yourself (see our Terms of Use).

**We don't collect:** your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, payment details, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics.

## 3. Permissions the app asks for

The app asks for these only when you first use the feature that needs them. You can say no, and you can change your mind at any time in your iPhone's **Settings › The Pantry**.

- **Camera.** Used only when you choose to take a photo of a dish you cooked ("Share a dish"). The photo is kept on your phone.
- **Photos.** Used only when you choose a photo from your library for a dish. The app gets a copy of the photos you pick, not your whole library. The originals in your library aren't changed.
- **Notifications.** Used for Cook Mode timers that finish while your phone is locked, and the optional Sunday planning reminder. These are scheduled on your phone itself. No notification comes from, or goes through, a server of ours.

If you say no, everything else in the app still works.

## 4. When information leaves your phone

The app doesn't send your information to us. It only leaves your phone when **you** choose to send it:

- **Sharing.** When you tap Share on your shopping list, your plan or a recipe, the app opens your iPhone's share sheet with that text. Where it goes (Messages, WhatsApp, Notes, email) is your choice, and that app's own privacy policy applies.
- **Importing a recipe from a web link.** When you paste a link and import it, your phone visits that web page directly, the same way your browser would, and reads the recipe from it. The website will see a normal visit from your phone, including its internet (IP) address, under that website's own privacy policy. The link and the recipe aren't sent to us.

**Apple.** Apple runs the App Store and iOS, and may give us summary statistics such as download numbers. If you've agreed in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also give us crash reports, which don't identify you. Apple's handling of this is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

## 6. How we keep it safe

Your information is held in The Pantry's private storage on your iPhone, which other apps can't read. It's protected by your phone's passcode and Face ID or Touch ID, and by Apple's device encryption. Keep your phone locked and up to date.

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes The Pantry's data. That backup is held by Apple or on your computer, under their settings, not by us.

## 7. Seeing, changing and deleting your information

Everything is in the app, so you can see and change it there at any time:

- Edit or remove cupboard items, plan entries, list items, saved recipes, collections, your own recipes and your plates directly where they appear.
- Change your diet, avoid list, cuisines and weeknight time in **Settings › Cooking**.
- Remove a plate (dish photo) from its page. The originals in your Photos library aren't affected.

**To delete everything,** delete The Pantry from your iPhone (press and hold the icon, then Remove App › Delete App). This removes all of the app's data from your phone. Copies in an older iCloud or computer backup stay there until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Because we don't hold any of your information, there's nothing for us to give you or delete on our side. You're still welcome to ask us about it at [contact email].
